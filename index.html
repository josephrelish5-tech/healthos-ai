SPECIALIST DEPARTMENT GUIDE
Department workspaces & responsibilities
Role-specific work guidance for Nursing, Laboratory, Radiology, Emergency Services and Medical Records. Your active department assignment and existing permissions continue to control which records and actions you can access.

 

  
NURSING
Nursing care & handover
Coordinate patient observations, nursing notes, care plans, medication administration documentation, shift handovers and escalation of changes in patient condition.
  • Confirm patient identity and active encounter.
  • Record observations with date, time and responsible staff member.
  • Escalate urgent changes using hospital protocol.
  • Document handovers and outstanding care tasks.
Use only the clinical forms and permissions enabled for your assigned role.

  
LABORATORY
Laboratory & diagnostic workflow
Coordinate test requests, authorisation, specimen collection, accessioning, processing, quality checks and result release.
  • Match every specimen to the correct patient and request.
  • Track collection, receipt and processing timestamps.
  • Record authorised results and critical-value escalation.
  • Reconcile completed tests against service capture.
No result should be invented, auto-released or substituted for laboratory verification.

  
RADIOLOGY
Imaging & radiology coordination
Manage imaging requests, patient preparation, modality scheduling, image acquisition, reporting and communication of urgent findings.
  • Verify request, patient identity and examination.
  • Record modality, operator and examination time.
  • Attach reports only after authorised review.
  • Escalate urgent findings through approved clinical channels.
This guide does not create a PACS or diagnostic image viewer.

  
EMERGENCY SERVICES
Emergency response & patient flow
Support rapid registration, triage handover, escalation, bed coordination and accurate time-stamped emergency documentation.
  • Never delay emergency care for software entry.
  • Record triage and escalation in line with clinical protocol.
  • Coordinate queue, bed and transfer status.
  • Keep a clear record of staff handovers.
The platform is not an emergency dispatch service or substitute for clinical judgement.

  
MEDICAL RECORDS
Health information & records
Maintain accurate patient registration, record indexing, authorised retrieval, document completeness and controlled release of information.
  • Use approved patient identity matching procedures.
  • Protect confidentiality and access audit trails.
  • Track missing or duplicate documentation for review.
  • Release records only with valid authority and consent.
Never share patient records through personal email or unapproved channels.

 


  
RECEPTION & PATIENT SERVICES
Reception, registration & patient navigation
Welcome patients and visitors, support registration, appointment check-in, queue direction and clear communication with the appropriate care team.
  • Confirm patient identity using approved procedures.
  • Check appointment and referral details.
  • Direct urgent presentations to the hospital's triage process without delay.
  • Protect private information at the front desk.
Reception staff should not make clinical triage decisions unless appropriately trained and authorised.

  
MATERNITY & MIDWIFERY
Maternity, antenatal & postnatal care
Coordinate maternal visits, admission readiness, midwifery documentation, newborn care records and follow-up under the hospital's approved clinical protocols.
  • Record maternal and newborn observations in the authorised clinical record.
  • Document care, handovers and escalation with timestamps.
  • Track antenatal and postnatal appointments.
  • Protect mother and newborn confidentiality.
Clinical assessment and emergency obstetric decisions remain with qualified professionals.

  
FINANCE & ADMINISTRATION
Finance, administration & hospital coordination
Support accountable hospital administration across finance, records, procurement and departmental coordination. Finance users should work only in their authorised finance workspace.
  • Reconcile invoices, receipts, POS records and bank evidence.
  • Separate transaction entry, approval, refund and reconciliation duties.
  • Record supporting evidence and approval references.
  • Escalate unmatched transactions and unexplained adjustments for review.
This photograph shows hospital staff, not a finance team specifically. Finance actions remain permission-controlled and evidence-based.


  
DENTAL SERVICES
Dental care & oral health
Coordinate dental appointments, oral-health assessments, treatment plans, procedure records and follow-up within the dental team's authorised workflow.
  • Verify patient identity and dental history.
  • Record examination findings and consent in the approved clinical record.
  • Document procedures, materials and responsible clinician.
  • Schedule follow-up and capture completed services for billing review.
Dental diagnosis and treatment decisions remain with registered dental professionals.

  
PHYSIOTHERAPY & REHABILITATION
Physiotherapy & rehabilitation
Organise rehabilitation referrals, functional assessments, therapy sessions, progress notes and discharge follow-up.
  • Record referral and authorised rehabilitation goals.
  • Document each session, interventions and patient response.
  • Track functional progress against clinician-defined goals.
  • Escalate new symptoms or safety concerns to the responsible clinician.
Therapy plans and progression require professional assessment and clinical oversight.

  
THEATRE & PERIOPERATIVE SERVICES
Operating theatre & perioperative workflow
Coordinate theatre scheduling, pre-operative checks, team readiness, procedure documentation, recovery handover and consumable accountability.
  • Confirm approved procedure, patient identity and consent checks.
  • Record theatre team, start and end times and procedure evidence.
  • Document instrument, implant and consumable usage under policy.
  • Complete recovery handover and reconcile performed services.
This is an operational coordination guide, not a substitute for surgical safety protocols or clinical judgement.

  
SOCIAL WELFARE & PATIENT SUPPORT
Social welfare, patient navigation & support
Coordinate non-clinical support referrals, patient navigation, approved assistance requests, discharge support and links to community services.
  • Record the patient's support request with appropriate consent.
  • Refer to authorised social-work or community-support personnel.
  • Track referral status and follow-up without exposing sensitive details.
  • Document assistance decisions and supporting evidence.
Access to sensitive social information must be restricted to authorised staff.

  
PUBLIC HEALTH & COMMUNITY OUTREACH
Public health & community services
Coordinate approved outreach activities, health education, screening events, immunisation support and community follow-up.
  • Plan outreach sessions and assign authorised personnel.
  • Record aggregate activity and approved individual encounters.
  • Track referrals and follow-up with consent and privacy safeguards.
  • Report programme outputs using verified source records.
Public-health reporting must follow applicable consent, confidentiality and reporting requirements.

 
These are department guidance cards, not newly activated clinical modules. Actual staff access, clinical forms, result entry, imaging storage and workflow actions remain governed by existing hospital configuration, role permissions and approved integrations.
